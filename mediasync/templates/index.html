Getting started

  1. Paste the URL of a YouTube playlist, channel or video above and press the download button. Playlists become a TV show in playlist order, channels a TV show with one season per year, single videos a movie.
  2. In Kodi, add as a TV shows source and as a Movies source, both with the scraper Local information only. (Jellyfin: add them as Shows and Movies libraries.)
  3. Press Update all now and then (or turn on automatic checks in Settings) to get new videos.

Each video is downloaded once. Later updates only fetch what is new or missing.

    Settings

    Libraries

    Each library has a Shows folder (channels and playlists; add it to Kodi as a TV shows source) and a Movies folder (single videos; a Movies source). The first one is the default for new downloads. A library on a drive that is not plugged in shows as offline and is left alone until you connect it.
